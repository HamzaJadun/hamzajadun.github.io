Dutch Bros Secret Menu

Fan-reported builds, not an official Dutch Bros menu. Order by describing the flavours, not just by the secret name — not every stand will know every name. Prices follow the base drink category in the main menu.

  • Shark Attack Rebel — Blue Rebel base, commonly reported as blue raspberry + coconut + lime, often with pomegranate drizzle.
  • Galaxy Fish — Rebel-based fan build, reported as a sweet / fruity Rebel variation. Ask for the flavour combination, not only the name.
  • Picture Perfect — Caramel + chocolate drizzle coffee build, a well-known Dutch Bros fan favourite.
  • Cocomo — Coconut + chocolate mocha-style build.
  • Double Torture — Extra-espresso build in the 9-1-1 family, for strong coffee orders.

Tip: secret drinks are customisations of base drinks. If a stand does not recognise a name, order the base drink (for example a Rebel, breve, or freeze) and list the syrups / toppings you want.
