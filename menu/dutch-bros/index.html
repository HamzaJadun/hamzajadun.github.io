FAQ

How much is a Dutch Bros drink?

Most drinks fall roughly between $2.50 and $7.00, depending on size and category. Dutch Soda and drip coffee are usually cheapest; large Rebel and freeze drinks are usually most expensive.

Are sugar-free syrups free?

Most listings report sugar-free syrup swaps as usually free. Extra espresso, alt-milk swaps, and Soft Top are usually about $0.50–$0.75 extra.

Is this the official Dutch Bros menu?

No. This is an independent fan / price-guide site. For exact prices, use the official Dutch Bros app or your local stand's menu board.
